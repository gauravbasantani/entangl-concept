# Entangl concept

A procedure step on iPad that stops the technician when the drawing changed after the procedure was written, shows what changed, and records what they did.

An unofficial concept by Gaurav Basantani (https://gauravbasantani.com), based only on public information. Not affiliated with Entangl. The site, equipment, procedure and people are made up, and the steps are simplified; this is not a real switching procedure.

Live page: https://gauravbasantani.github.io/entangl-concept/
